SCOPE AND LIMITATIONS

Evaluation limitations

  • Synthetic claims use selected paraphrases of public California sample policy forms; declarations are fictional and policy terms are not exhaustive.
  • Expected labels are illustrative judgments, not expert-reviewed ground truth. One rollout; no held-out validation.
  • Current 15-case set: 5 covered, 4 excluded, 6 requiring review; majority-label baseline 40%. Original five-case baseline: 60%.
  • Luna confidence is not calibrated; refresh its results with the documented command after a new conversation run.
  • Conversations may omit scenario facts. Scores evaluate the conversation and vision-to-decision workflow, not an isolated model; expected labels require expert review.
  • Harbor checks Luna verdict labels only; Astra clause ID checks do not prove semantic support.
  • Images are synthetic; Harbor replays saved Luna outputs and does not make new model calls.
  • ClaimBench is a financial 10-K statement verification reference, not an insurance benchmark. ClaimLab adopts fixed labels, evidence-only reasoning, insufficient-information handling, confidence reporting, and latency reporting, with no equivalent scores.
